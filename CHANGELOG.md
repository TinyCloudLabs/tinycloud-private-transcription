# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Integration fixtures restore `HOME` and `TMPDIR` after their file-local scratch directories are used. Details: TC-2181

- TC-2181: a Google Meet link in any pasted shape starts a meeting. `POST /v1/meetings` rewrites `meet.google.com/meet/<code>`, `/_meet/<code>`, `g.co/meet/<code>`, `google.com/meet/<code>`, `tel.meet/<code>`, a `google.com/url?q=`, `accounts.google.com?continue=` or `meet.app.goo.gl?link=` wrapper, and a bare code (with or without dashes) to `https://meet.google.com/<code>` (lowercase, no trailing slash) before the platform is detected; the stored URL and the idempotency hash use that form. Only the Google Meet hosts are recognised; a wrapper whose target is not a Meet link is refused. `meet.google.com/lookup/<nick>` is accepted and sent to the bot as pasted (no native meeting id). Other links are unchanged. Details: TC-2181
- TC-2128: retained-recording Whisper transcription bounds each encoded WAV to 580 seconds, including a two-second shared seam, with one FFmpeg decode and sample views. Quiet cuts cover the full recording; segment offsets are restored to the recording clock and duplicate boundary words are removed only where their audio spans overlap. A seam consumes one matching suffix/prefix across segment boundaries: a previous occurrence cannot erase multiple incoming repetitions, and differing segment partitions do not leave duplicate seam words. A failed upload still fails the attempt instead of publishing a transcript with a hole. Oversized attributed/recording windows retain the existing sub-30-second timestamp convention, including padding in the limit. Model selection and defaults are unchanged by this change; local controlled ASR verification does not establish real-Whisper speech quality or authorize a default switch.
- TC-2148: the meeting transcript path drops Whisper sound captions too. `hallucinationReason` returns the new reason `caption` when the whole text of a piece is one wrapped sound or music caption (`*Piano music*`, `[Music]`, `(applause)`, `♪ music ♪`), using the same closed list as ptx-batch (TC-2144). It applies to recording gap-fill windows and pieces, retained-recording recovery chunks and attributed batches, and is not gated on a model name because the meeting path uses Whisper only since TC-2118. A dropped caption is a listed gap (recovery: the transcript is marked `partial`), as for any other dropped text over possible speech; a caption inside longer text, unlisted bracketed text and a bare `Music` are kept. Checked after every existing rule, so no existing result changes. No new log event and no Vector or log-shipper change (`reason` is not a shipped enum field on the meeting path). Takes effect on the next API and worker deploy.
- TC-2144: for Whisper-class models ptx-batch also drops a region whose whole text is one wrapped sound or music caption (`*Piano music*`, `[Music]`, `(applause)`, `♪ music ♪`, `[BLANK_AUDIO]`), as no speech. The match is a closed list of sound words (at most 40 characters inside the wrapper); a caption inside longer text (`[laughs] and then he left`), unlisted bracketed text and a bare `Music` are kept. Counted by the existing `batch region ungrounded` event; no new event, no Vector change. Voxtral is unchanged. Takes effect on the next ptx-batch deploy (drain first).
- TC-2118: no audio or chat LLM transcribes meeting speech. The attributed Google Meet path used `TINFOIL_ATTRIBUTED_FALLBACK_MODEL` (`voxtral-small-24b`) for a batch, or recording gap-fill chunk, after two empty Whisper results, and an assistant-style reply to unclear audio ("Thank you for your question. However, I'm not sure what you're referring to...") was published as speech. The setting, the model switch and `source: "fallback"` output are removed (no replacement flag). A batch Whisper answers empty twice now stops retrying and is gap-filled from the retained recording by Whisper, then listed in `gaps`; nothing is invented. `TINFOIL_ATTRIBUTED_MODEL` now defaults to `whisper-large-v3-turbo` instead of the recovery model. Last-line guard (`src/domain/hallucination.ts`, every provider and audio length): text that is wholly assistant boilerplate (two or more sentences, all courtesy or reply sentences, at least one reply sentence) is rejected, including a reply split across Whisper segments; untimed text is also rejected when its speaking rate is implausible for the voiced seconds. Results already stored by the fallback model are published as gaps, not text. The `infra/dstack` compose drops the variable; ptx-batch is unchanged.
- TC-2118: retained-recording recovery (`TINFOIL_MODEL`, used when Vexa lost a material tail) now defaults to `whisper-large-v3-turbo` instead of `voxtral-small-24b`, in `config.ts`, `infra/dstack/app-compose.yaml` (api and worker) and both `.env.example` files. Its text now passes the same last-line guard as the attributed path: a chunk that is an assistant-style reply, a loop, or an implausible speaking rate for its voiced seconds is dropped and the transcript is marked `partial`; if every chunk is dropped the recovery fails with no words, as for an empty answer. A sealed `TINFOIL_MODEL` overrides the compose default: remove it, or set it to a Whisper model. `EVAL_MODELS` shadow runs and ptx-batch (`BATCH_TINFOIL_MODEL`) are unchanged.
- TC-2119: ptx-batch drops a region whose whole returned text is chat-assistant boilerplate (for example "Thank you for your question. However, I'm not sure what you're referring to. Could you please provide more context…"), so an audio-LLM answer is not published as speech. Only short text made entirely of assistant phrases is dropped; speech that merely contains a phrase is kept. A job whose regions are all dropped ends `no_speech`. New log event `batch_region_ungrounded` (`dropped_regions`, count only, no text) is allowlisted in the log shipper; the embedded Vector config in `infra/dstack-batch/app-compose.yaml` changes, so this needs a ptx-batch CVM redeploy.
- TC-2115: the batch worker logs a `batch worker heartbeat` (info) at start and every 60 s with four integers: `queued_jobs`, `processing_jobs`, `awaiting_upload_jobs` and `oldest_queued_age_s` (seconds the oldest claimable queued job has waited, from the database clock; 0 when none). Counts only, no ids. One indexed statement over the active rows; a failed query logs the existing `batch worker heartbeat failed`. The Vector allowlist (`fields.json`, regenerated `vector.yaml` and `app-compose.yaml`) maps the message to the existing `batch_worker_heartbeat` event and ships the four fields on that event only, each with a strict integer range. The compose changes, so the CVM needs a redeploy.
- TC-2128: ptx-batch transcribes with `whisper-large-v3-turbo` by default (the meeting path's transcriber); `BATCH_TINFOIL_MODEL=voxtral-small-24b` switches back. For Whisper-class models (name starts with `whisper`) a region whose whole text is a silence caption (`you`, `thank you for watching`, `thanks for watching`, `bye`, `bye bye`, `subtitles by the amara.org community`, or only punctuation) is dropped as no speech; a lone `thank you` is kept. No chunking is added: every batch region is at most 30 s (`VAD.maxMs`), so no request approaches Whisper's 600 s input limit.
- TC-2084: batch jobs wait instead of failing when the transcription provider refuses for billing, quota or key reasons. 402, 401, 403 (and 429 beyond the consecutive cap) release the job to `queued` with a durable not-before (backoff 1, 2, 5, 10, 30 min, then every 30 min, up to 24 h from the first block, then `provider_unavailable`); no retry counter is consumed and the upload is kept. The job status carries `provider_delayed: true` while it waits. New log events `batch_provider_blocked` (`http_status`, `reason`) and `batch_provider_recovered` (`delayed_jobs`) are allowlisted in the log shipper. Ambiguous outcomes (5xx, timeout, reset) keep the no-retry rule; 404 stays terminal. Migration `0028` adds the additive table `transcription_provider_blocks`.
- Dev stack (`infra/dstack`): optional, digest-pinned debug sidecars: key-only SSH through the dstack gateway (`debug-ssh`; its one public key is inlined in the measured compose, placeholder `REPLACE_AT_DEPLOY` refuses to start), a Vector log shipper to the Better Stack source `ptx-dev` that ships only an allowlist of containers (meeting bots, `meeting-api`, `gateway`, `api`, `worker`, `probe`, `debug-ssh`; `debug-log-shipper`), and an on-demand read-only status probe (`profiles: ["probe"]`). A misconfigured `debug-ssh` refuses to start and restarts in a back-off loop; `debug-log-shipper` without a token logs one line and idles. `infra/dstack/deploy-ptx-dev.sh` updates the CVM only while it runs the pinned production OS image `dstack-0.5.12`, re-checks it by CVM id after the deploy and after the `allowed_envs` sync, and exits non-zero on any failure. See `infra/dstack/DEBUG.md`.
- TC-922: batch uploads advertise nominal three-hour support (`10800` seconds) with an inclusive raw ffprobe ceiling of `11101` seconds and a shared `186496800`-byte cap. Reject over-ceiling raw duration before rounding, align Bun's body limit, and retain the full accepted tail in worker decode. Default reserved/daily byte budgets become `1864968000`/`559490400`; processing/upload/PUT deadlines and provider behavior are unchanged. Long-recording validation scope is MP3/AAC only; WAV remains byte-capped. See [evidence and caveats](docs/tc-922-validation.md).
- TC-1093: diarized batch jobs retain ordinary VAD fallback coverage even when quiet detection's peak gate misses or quiet mode is disabled. Known speaker turns remain intact; newly covered audio uses the existing conservative unknown/sliver attribution, without duplicate audio or requests over 30 seconds.
- Apply the existing floor-coverage bound to final diarized turns before persisting regions or calling the provider. Primary VAD coverage can repair diarizer omissions; an insufficient final union fails `processing_failed` instead of publishing a partial transcript. Result mode/fallback and drop metrics describe the selected mode and final coverage, and remain atomic with saved regions across re-claims.
- Retain deterministic short/fail-closed regressions and a manual public-AMI pre-deploy HTTP API/worker replay in `test/support/diarized-replay.ts`. The replay uses the shipped native diarizer and validates every dispatched WAV against captured worker PCM; its loopback provider returns deterministic text, so it does not measure ASR accuracy or hallucinations. The short bypass existed at both `7e54bcd2` and #105; this is not a claim that #105 caused it.
